Summary

MoE (35B total, ~3B active) + MTP is the new speed king: 68 py / 74 js tok/s at draft depth 3 — 1.5× Gemma-12B, 4× dense Qwen3.8 — with the strongest base-model coding pedigree tested (73.4 SWE-bench Verified). Single-session context reaches 136K at ~65 tok/s (24.5 GB RSS); the ceiling is the 20 GB of weights, not KV (~19 KB/token — decode speed is flat across the whole range). Quality gate (EvalPlus) pending.
