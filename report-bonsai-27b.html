mlx-lm 0.31.3 (llama-server pending a release) · prism-ml ternary 2-bit · benchmarked 2026-08-25

Summary

Ternary (2-bit) Bonsai — PrismML's quality-oriented compression of Qwen3.6-27B, claiming 95% of full-precision performance — decodes at 28.6 tok/s on mlx-lm with only ~8 GB of weights, and holds 96K context (26.4 GB peak; the model window is 262K but memory caps below it). Same context reach as Qwen3.8-GGUF, 70% faster decode. The GGUF variant is on disk but needs a llama.cpp newer than the current brew release — parked until then. EvalPlus verified: 0.915 / 0.884 HumanEval+ (fair budget, night 2) — 27B-class quality from 8 GB of weights.

Fair score — the token-budget flaw is fixed. Night 2 calibrated the budget (10240) and regenerated all 55 budget-truncated completions: the score jumped from night 1's 0.640/0.634 to 0.915/0.884, the biggest correction of any model. The remaining 5 empty completions are a real model ceiling (still empty at the full budget), not a harness artifact. The ternary claim holds up: 2-bit compression kept near-27B-class quality. Practical note: Bonsai is the least disruptive model to work alongside (moderate fan noise, low memory footprint) — a good all-day background agent. Details in night2/results.md.

Blocked / parked

GGUF Q2_0 (6.7 GB, on disk, byte-verified): the current brew llama.cpp (build 10621) predates the final Q2_0 tensor layout and refuses to load it. Parked until the next stable release — that unlocks llama-server slots, preallocated context, and a possible multi-slot story. When starting it, pin the file: --hf-file Ternary-Bonsai-27B-Q2_0.gguf (the :Q2_0 tag wrongly matches the PQ2_0 variant).

Method: warmup before every measurement; identical prompts across models; temp 0. Full raw numbers in benchmarks-bonsai-27b.md. 1-bit variants were dropped from scope and deleted.
