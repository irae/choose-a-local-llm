Decision: parallel serving runs on llama-server only. MLX has no slots; its only concurrency is one server process per agent, each with its own full weight copy and its own port to wire into the harness. Measured (two Bonsai instances: 14.0 tok/s each, 14.9 GB) but ruled out as not worth the operational fiddling. Bonsai regains a multi-session story when a brew llama.cpp release loads its ternary GGUF (projected ~300K total context to split across slots).
