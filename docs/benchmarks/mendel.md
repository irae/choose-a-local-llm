#### Legend

- **Score**: out of 100, capped at the share of libraries done. A
  muted percentage is that share.
- **Wall**: minutes from the first prompt to the end; the cap is 300.
- **Ctx / speed**: the harness window, over the config's real-text
  decode speed, shallow then deep. A dagger: the config now serves a
  larger window.
- **Tokens**: tokens the model generated.
- **Ctx use**: peak context as a share of the window, plus 100 percent
  per compaction.
- **Bugs**: defects by severity.
- **Stats**: `calls` tool calls / failed; `commits`; `turns` assistant
  messages; `nudges`, `t` tooling cause, `m` model cause; `loop` a
  repetition the runner stopped; `trimmed` the share of noisy commands
  the model trimmed.
- Bold: the best two of a column, and any row within 15 percent of the
  column's span of the second best.
