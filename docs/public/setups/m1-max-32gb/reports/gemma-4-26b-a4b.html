Summary

The MoE big sibling of Gemma-12B does everything its family does, faster. Under the current wired limit of 25000 the full 256K trained window still fits on one slot — but only with q8_0 KV (f16 now OOMs). q8 costs js speed: draft acceptance falls from 81% to 68%, so js decodes at ~53 tok/s vs py's 62-68. Two slots reach 2×184K. Thinking (binary, default off) costs only ~3 tok/s. Quality gate (EvalPlus) pending.
